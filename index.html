Frequently Asked Questions

How long does setup take?

Setup takes less than 5 minutes. Simply connect your CRM, select your Slack channel, and you're done. First insights arrive next Monday.

Which CRMs do you support?

We currently support Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, and Airtable. More integrations coming soon!

Can I customize the reports?

Yes! You can customize which metrics to track, alert thresholds, and report frequency through our Streamlit dashboard.

Is my data secure?

Absolutely. We use enterprise-grade encryption, never store raw CRM data, and are SOC 2 compliant. Your data stays in your CRM; we only analyze it.

Our Brand Story

Transforming sales analytics with AI innovation

PipelinePulse was born from a simple observation: sales teams spend countless hours buried in CRM dashboards, manually compiling reports, and still missing critical insights. We knew there had to be a better way.

Founded in 2024, our mission is to make business intelligence conversational, automated, and actionable. We believe that powerful analytics shouldn't require a data science degree or hours of manual work.

Today, PipelinePulse serves 50+ companies, saving sales teams an average of 15 hours per week while increasing pipeline visibility by 89%. Our AI-powered platform delivers insights directly to where teams work—no dashboard required.
